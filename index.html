Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Retro Beat ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app collects, uses, and protects information when you use Retro Beat.

Information We Collect

The app may access the following information and permissions:

  • Music & Audio Files – To scan, organize, and play music stored on your device.
  • Microphone – Only when you choose to record voice overlays or audio recordings.
  • Camera – Only when you use QR code scanning features for Party Connect.
  • Notifications – To provide media playback controls and playback status notifications.
  • Storage Access – To export audio files, create ringtones, save recordings, and manage user-generated content.

Advertising

Retro Beat may display advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process certain device identifiers and usage information in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

Learn more:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Audio Studio Features

Audio Studio features such as trimming, exporting, ringtone creation, and voice overlays are processed locally on your device. Audio files are not uploaded to our servers.

Party Connect

Party Connect operates over your local network. Music synchronization and related communication occur directly between participating devices. We do not store Party Connect session data on external servers.

Data Storage

Playlists, favorites, lyrics, audio presets, exported files, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

The app may use:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

Children's Privacy

Retro Beat is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
