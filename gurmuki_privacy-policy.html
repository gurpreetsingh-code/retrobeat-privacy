Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Gurmukhi Keyboard AI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects information.

Information We Collect

Gurmukhi Keyboard AI is designed to operate primarily on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal typing data to our servers.

Keyboard Input

Text entered using the keyboard remains on your device. We do not store, monitor, or share sensitive information such as passwords, messages, credit card details, or personal data.

Voice Input

When using voice typing, speech recognition services provided by your device or platform may process audio input. Please review the privacy policies of those providers for additional information.

Handwriting Recognition

Handwriting recognition is processed locally on your device using on-device machine learning technologies whenever possible. Your handwriting data is not uploaded to our servers.

Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Microphone Permission – Required for voice typing.
  • Internet Permission – Required for downloading language or handwriting recognition models and app updates.

Data Storage

Personal dictionary entries, keyboard preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party technologies such as Google ML Kit and Android speech recognition services to provide handwriting and voice recognition functionality. These services operate according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
